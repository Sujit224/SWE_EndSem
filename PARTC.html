Agile vs Waterfall

Why we chose Agile for SwiftRide

01
🏆

Superior Methodology

Agile overscores Waterfall because it is flexible, customer-centric, faster, collaborative and making it ideal for modern projects.

02
🔄

High Flexibility

Agile is so Flexible and adaptable when compared to Waterfall method.

03
📝

Adaptable Requirements

Requirement changes can be pushed in later part of the development.

04
🤝

Continuous Communication

Communication with Client and Engineering team is Continuous which reflects positive attitude to the team.

05
🔬

Early Risk Detection

Small increments make it easier to test, adjust, and detect risks early.

06
✅

Continuous Testing

Continuous testing, regular reviews, frequent integration where in testing in waterfall happened after the development phase.

07
🚀

Frequent Releases

Frequent small releases where in waterfall delivers at the end.

08
🛡️

Risk Mitigation

In Agile Risk can be identified early and gets mitigated where in waterfall Risk is identified after testing phase.
